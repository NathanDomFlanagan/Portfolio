How to Play Snake

Use the keys to move the snake (or the on-screen arrows on touch devices):

  • Up: W
  • Down: S
  • Left: A
  • Right: D

Press E — or tap ⏯ on touch — to pause / start.

Eat the red food to grow and score.

Avoid walls and yourself.
